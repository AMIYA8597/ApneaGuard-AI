# AASM Sleep Apnea Severity Bands

The severity of obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) is clinically evaluated using the Apnea-Hypopnea Index (AHI). The AHI is calculated by dividing the total number of apnea and hypopnea events by the total hours of sleep.

According to the American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM) standards, the severity thresholds are defined as follows:

| Severity Category | AHI (Events per Hour) | Description |
|-------------------|-----------------------|-------------|
| **Normal** | < 5 | Fewer than 5 events per hour. Typically considered within normal physiological limits. |
| **Mild** | 5 to < 15 | Between 5 and 14.9 events per hour. |
| **Moderate** | 15 to < 30 | Between 15 and 29.9 events per hour. |
| **Severe** | ≥ 30 | 30 or more events per hour. |

### Source Citation
These standardized thresholds are established by the American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM) and are widely cited by clinical bodies including the NIH and the Cleveland Clinic.
